03 / The person behind the code

Always building.
Always becoming.

Priyanshu AgrawalEngineer. Learner. Fellow human.

I'm a full-stack engineer based in Bengaluru, India. My work spans large-scale distributed systems, web experiences, and the developer tools that make building them a little easier.

These days, I'm a Principal Software Engineer at Microsoft on Power Pages, within Power Platform. I lead agentic authoring and work on pro-developer tools, including PAC CLI and the Power Platform Tools VS Code extension.

That work spans the Power Pages AI plugin, SPA sites, samples, and templates. Before moving to Power Pages, I worked on Bing's frontend platform and Copilot testing, with earlier chapters at GE Digital and Infosys.

I enjoy learning new technologies, following a good question, and turning what I learn into something useful. I also share that work with the community as a Power Platform Community Conference speaker.
